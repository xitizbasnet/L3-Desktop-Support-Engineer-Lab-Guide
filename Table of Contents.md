## Labs

### Lab 1: Kernel, Driver & OS-Level Troubleshooting

Advanced troubleshooting of kernel, driver, and operating-system-level issues.

### Lab 2: Advanced Log & Memory Dump Analysis

Advanced analysis of system logs and memory dumps to identify and diagnose complex issues.

### Lab 3: Endpoint Management Strategy Design

Design and evaluation of endpoint management strategies for enterprise environments.

### Lab 4: Vendor Collaboration & Patch Management

Management of vendor interactions and enterprise patch-management activities.

### Lab 5: PowerShell Automation for Desktop Support

Development and application of PowerShell automation to improve desktop support operations.

### Lab 6: SCCM & Intune Configuration Management

Configuration and management of enterprise endpoints using **SCCM** and **Microsoft Intune**.

### Lab 7: Incident Documentation & Root Cause Analysis

Professional incident documentation and structured root cause analysis for complex technical issues.
